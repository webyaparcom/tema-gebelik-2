Questions

Questions along the way

Content is for general information only and does not replace an examination by a doctor or midwife. In an emergency call 112.

  1. 1

    Getting started

    2
    In which week should I start the school?add

    Groups usually start after week 20; discuss the right time with your doctor.

    How does the first talk work?add

    In person, by video or by phone for 30 minutes; we talk about your expectations and the right group.

  2. 2

    Classes

    2
    Can my partner join?add

    Yes; a support person can join every session without a separate registration.

    What if I miss a session?add

    You receive a written summary and can catch up with the next group.

  3. 3

    Health

    2
    Does the school replace pregnancy check-ups?add

    No. The school provides information; examinations, tests and decisions stay with your doctor.

    What if I feel unwell during a session?add

    The session pauses; a midwife looks after you and calls 112 if needed. With an urgent symptom call 112 without waiting for the school.
